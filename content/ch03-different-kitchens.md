# 03 · Different kitchens

Short projects, different roles that widened my view of F&B

## [Golden Gate JSC](https://ggg.com.vn/)

Marketing Assistant to the Brand Manager · Contract · Mar – Jul 2024

Grand Opening Campaign for [Sakura Yakiniku](https://www.facebook.com/sakurayakiniku.official)

- Developed marketing proposals for a range of Japanese brands.
- Built brand strategy frameworks and launch/relaunch campaign plans.
- Produced video content, and wrote editing guidelines for the team.

- Learned to plan and allocate a large campaign budget.
- Learned to coordinate with the Northern and Southern regional teams, keeping brand voice and visuals consistent across markets.

At this scale, the job is less about shaping a brand and more about keeping it in line with the budget, targets, and overall scale.

## Lam Yên & 123 Restaurant

Operations Assistant to the Brand Owner · Part-time · Sep 2024 – Jun 2025

- **Lam Yên** Joined operational meetings and took on responsibilities across different areas, especially during peak seasons (order distribution, queue management,…)
- **{{123 Restaurant}}** Alongside operational support, I also took on **social media marketing** for the restaurant for a period of time. One of the highlights was a TikTok initiative.

Different roles got me thinking beyond marketing.

## Mono Coffee Lab

Marketing Specialist (project-based) · Contract · May – Oct 2025

If the heart of Lam Yên is the product, the heart of Mono is the service.

Mono is already good at expressing its brand spirit through warm service and authentic stories on social media – _an energetic, warm-hearted friend._

Beyond the experience, Mono also has a strong offering of house-made products, from coffee and cakes to brunch.

The brief: Make Mono’s house-made products more appealing and bring them closer to its guests.

- **On social:** real product photos shot in the café, more polished but still in Mono’s own setting
- **In the café:** flexible POSM on the tables, an iPad at the cashier, new menu experiences
- **Summer Brunch launch:** guest opinion cards in store first, then the social push

_Creative Direction & Design Refinement_

_Mono Brunch Menu (Draft)_

Here, a customer meets the brand at every step: the table, the menu, the cashier, the staff,…

It’s not just **a shelf of products** like at Lam Yên. Mono filled in **a part of my marketing approach that was missing.**

And it showed me that what works for one brand doesn’t necessarily work for another.

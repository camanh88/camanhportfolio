# 01 · It started with culture

A culture lover who wanted to become a marketer

**Marketing Executive · In-house photographer**

{{Indochine House}} · Full-time · Aug 2023 – Feb 2024

In search of connection with Hanoi’s people and culture, I found my beginning in **art**.

As part of the in-house marketing team of a commercial gallery, my brief was never “sell this piece.” It was to ++carry the spirit, meaning and value of the works++ to the people who might one day live with them.

## ACCULTURATION

Art in the Flow of Culture

**The mission:**

- Produce the catalogue 03
- Launch it on social media
- Organise the Acculturation exhibitions

**My part: Imagery & Photography**

- Shaped the campaign’s big idea with the team
- Built the moodboards
- Ran the shoots, with the team’s support
- Produced images and short videos for social
- Pitched in on the exhibitions

## Highlights

**Studio visits.** I documented artists’ creative processes and listened to the stories behind their work. Their ideas were often abstract. My job was to turn them into something you could see, then decide where each piece should live.

**Two living spaces** We showcased the artworks in two distinct homes: **an Indochine villa** and **a modern apartment**, to demonstrate how art can live naturally across spaces, **from traditional architecture to contemporary interiors.**

**Catalogue 03.**

**In the gallery** Back in the gallery, we kept it simple: a classic layout, fresh flowers, and room for guests to move, talk and connect.

## Closing

Art trained my eye and fed my soul. It taught me to give form to ideas that don’t have one yet.

And yes, all of it was exquisite, and so tranquil.

But my true dream was…

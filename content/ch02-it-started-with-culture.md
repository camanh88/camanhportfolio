# 02 · It started with culture

A culture lover who wanted to become a marketer

Marketing Executive · In-house photographer

{{Indochine House}} · Full-time · Aug 2023 – Feb 2024

In search of connection with Hanoi's people and culture, I found my beginning in art. As part of the in-house marketing team of a commercial gallery, my brief was never "sell this piece." It was to carry the spirit, meaning and value of the works to the people who might one day live with them.

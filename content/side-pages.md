## About Cốm

BUILD NOTE: IMAGES END side-ly-2

**Cốm**, or young green rice flakes, is a traditional delicacy from Northern Vietnam, deeply associated with the arrival of autumn in Hanoi. Traditionally made in the autumn harvest, this humble gift from the land carries a fresh, grassy fragrance and a delicate sweetness.

Wrapped in lotus leaves and enjoyed a pinch at a time, Cốm invites you to slow down and savour its soft, chewy texture and subtle nutty, buttery notes.

02

My love language: gifting

Somewhere along the way of growing up, I noticed how I love people. Family, friends, love: it’s always the same. I give. I found out my love language is gifting, especially good food 😋

Honestly, many of my best relationships started with a box of cake. I’d invite someone to try Lam Yên, or just hand them a piece of something I truly love.In a way, I was sharing a piece of myself, and receiving one in return.

I once read a book called You and I Eat the Same, and one passage stuck with me.

I love gifting because there’s so much in it.

Sharing.

Caring.

Saying “I love you” without saying it.

Sometimes it’s a reason to keep in touch, a way to say hello or goodbye…

We don’t just give a gift.

We create a connection.

And with food, well, the shortest way to someone’s heart is through their stomach ;)

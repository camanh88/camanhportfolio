Why Marou?

Beyond loving chocolate…

It’s easy to say I love chocolate. I do!

But loving chocolate isn’t enough to truly belong on a team.

What brought me here is that I found my own values in Marou’s.

  • 01

    We’re all gift-oriented.

  • 02

    We all value quality, and we never compromise on it.

  • 03

    We’re all passionate and adventurous.

When the opportunity at Lam Yên came, I actually chose it over my plan to study abroad. It wasn’t easy, but it was a beautiful journey.

Today, I’ve packed up my life and moved to Saigon in search of a new “home” that shares my dream or perhaps an even bigger one: going global. And for me, that answer is Marou.

Marou has already built something people can taste and hold. With the team, I’d love to take it further: creating not just a 360° chocolate experience, but a brand people feel connected to, remember, and share as the best chocolate in Asia,and as the sweetest way to connect one person to another.

By gifting a gift that’sTASTING GOOD,we can beCONNECTING GOOD.

Let’s share “a chocolate meal” with the world ;)
